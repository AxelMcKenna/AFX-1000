# Physical validation of the repaired assemblies

This matrix covers E12-R2, P03, E28 and the E23I2 inlet in the A12 assembly. It is a development test specification, not a record of measurements or permission to manufacture an unfinished PCB. The original [Q02 packet](../../qualification/boards/README.md) still targets the original pictured boards; its captures cannot be relabeled as these replacements. There are currently **zero physical measurements** of the repaired assemblies.

Freeze the final native PCB/schematic/BOM, filled copper, accepted fabrication stack, harness and mechanical drawings before binding a physical capture packet. Native ERC, DRC, connectivity and schematic parity must be closed on that exact source. Identify each actual board by serial and as-built part lots, including substitutions. Preserve raw captures alongside the measured results, instrument identities/calibration and positive expanded uncertainty. A complete uncertainty interval must fit inside the applicable acceptance interval. A missing numerical limit remains unresolved; an absolute maximum or typical graph does not supply one.

| Test group | Actual connections and required evidence | Acceptance and unresolved inputs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unpowered inspection | All four boards: compare actual packages, polarity, terminal numbering, pad/mask/paste, holes, mounting, lead trim, BOM and independent continuity with the frozen design. Inspect both populated faces. | Zero wrong/missing parts, unintended shorts or land-pattern mismatches. Record the inspection method and fabrication/as-built traceability. This does not establish powered performance. |
| Fabrication construction | Microsections and certificates for the actual P03/E28 copper sheets, plated barrels, registration, finished bores, predrill geometry and filled/capped via-in-pad process. Inspect dense EP/WCSP fields and the exact TI RPY pad spacing. | P03's proposed 50 µm minimum wall / 85 µm outer / 70 µm inner copper is a custom development request at 1.6 mm total thickness. Its maximum dimensions, drill web, annulus after registration/etch and supplier acceptance remain open. Resin fill receives no solid-copper thermal credit. E28 has its own stack; do not substitute one board's certificate for the other. |
| Copper and return network | Four-wire resistance measurements at actual operating terminals and Kelvin taps, with warm/cold fixtures and independent current excitation. Include P03 J101.2, J20/J21 and the analog regulator/reference returns, not an ideal common source node. Measure E28 complete terminal/pad/branch copper and each protected output. | Source regulator-to-positive-stud path ≤1 mΩ; each complete positive link ≤2 mΩ; E28 branch copper and complete return-bar assemblies have separate model allocations. Bind every measured path to its exact endpoints. Recompute the signed multiport ground-transfer model from the actual geometry and currents; do not infer a quiet reference from a net label or cancel independent positive/negative loads. |
| Complete input chain | Actual GST60A12-P1J plug, PJ-102AH, mandatory rear 6 A fuse, both VH mating assemblies, wire/crimps, inlet/P03 copper and input guard. Simultaneously capture plug, DC_IN, protected input, loop current and temperatures. | The adapter's conditional static plug interval is 11.43–12.57 V; its cable drop is already included at that boundary. The 0.36 Ω complete-feed scenario remains unmeasured. Demonstrate the required protected-input minimum through steady load, startup and load steps with uncertainty. Catalog contact figures do not prove hot/lifetime assembled resistance. Fuse/adapter/TVS fault-energy coordination remains open. |
| Source and endpoint DC | P03 isolated source corners use the declared 9.8–13.2 V protected-input scenario; the complete installed adapter chain is a separate test. Exercise 0–40°C unit ambient, independent load combinations and actual local returns. Measure all six source rails and every final protected load at its device pins. | Use the final source-bound endpoint windows, including 4.975–5.025 V analog and 4.9–5.1 V digital targets. The protected loads include 2.4 A digital and 0.251 A analog; retained bipolar and regulator/control return currents must come from the final complete current ledger. A measurement at a convenient header does not qualify a remote endpoint. |
| Converter loops and startup | Both TPS543620 stages, both LT3471 converters and all retained linear/reference loops: record actual hot-loop geometry, effective capacitor/ESR corners, startup, load steps, dropout, shutdown and restart. Capture the local IC supply/feedback/ground nodes with documented probe loading. | Define allowable excursions, settling, stability margin and restart conditions before comparison. Review the U13 input bulk against the actual L6/L7 energy-current loops; distance to the IC's control-supply pin alone does not establish loop quality. A typical soft-start time or nominal capacitor value is not a guaranteed transient bound. |
| Positive disconnect sequencing | Capture each digital master/follower, analog master/followers, SPLYGD, EN/UV/OV, fault latch, POWER_RUN and actual downstream current. Exercise independent source loss, OV/UV, reference loss, AUX ramp/loss, fault recovery and the required manual cycle. | First master charges the entire downstream capacitance; do not divide it by the final parallel count. The 7.1 mF digital / 2.2 mF analog scenarios still need a complete effective-capacitance bound. Until every downstream load is actually held off, include the full constant load during ramp. Derive transient junction/SOA bounds from the actual current/voltage waveform and starting temperature; typical shutdown curves do not establish them. |
| Reverse and retained bipolar faults | All four retained Si8751 / CSD18540Q5B channels, plus the new positive protection: capture both MOSFET gates/common source, body-diode paths, input/output voltage and current under charged output, late closure, missing input and partial AUX. | Establish hot gate-drive/leakage, interruption/latency, residual-energy/discharge and device SOA limits for each channel. TPS259482L RCBCTRL is intentionally low in this candidate, so steady reverse blocking must not be presumed. Fault-source voltage, current, inductive energy and duration remain fixture-specific unresolved inputs. |
| Control receiver compatibility | Actual P03 J106 ↔ E28 control cable and every J9 OUTPUT_READY/FAULT/MASTER receiver, including reset/enable/unmute paths under partial power. | E28 status logic uses 2.5 V AUX. Prove receiver VIH/VIL, loading and power-order compatibility or implement translation. Do not assume a 3.3 V CMOS receiver accepts it. Readiness is not audio unmute or precision rail health. |
| ADC complete analog behavior | E12-R2 with the actual converter package/land/input/reference interface, force/sense wiring and maximum permitted cable parasitics. Exercise both signal paths, common-mode/reference servos, negative-bias pump, PD/clamps, missing bias and every supply-order/partial-power state. | Actual ESS package/input/reference/injection data remain missing. Measure coupled-loop stability, settling, leakage, gain, offset, distortion and noise against explicit subsystem allocations. The clean stage-only TI simulations do not qualify the complete analog chain; the assisted coupled simulations supply no accepted numerical comparison. Analog partial-power isolation remains a design hold. |
| Thermal assembly | Closed A12 housing with the actual metal base, feet/table clearance, source/load corners, TIM lot/compression, supported PCB and installed wiring. Measure every hot package and the local lands/base while recording ambient and electrical power balance. | Apply the [thermal bench requirements](../../cad/a12/thermal/bench-requirements.json): local base allocation ≤60°C; U1/U13 conservative project junction allocation ≤85°C including positive expanded uncertainty. Remaining packages need their own paths/operating bounds. No unlanded device inherits a neighbor's TIM path. Typical conductivity and a nominal process-trial pass do not establish a guaranteed junction bound. |
| Mechanical and service behavior | Manufactured mounting heights/bow, countersinks, thread engagement, complete mating/operating wire routes, terminal insulation/covers, retention and reassembly. Record return-bar alignment, reaction forces and resistance before/after relevant temperature/mechanical cycles. | The input-wire crossover currently clears stated conditional envelopes; manufacturer exit dimensions, forming fixture and strain relief remain unapproved. Positive-link/protected-output routes are unfinished. Fit bars to measured terminal planes without forced bending. React terminal-tightening torque with a fixture, rather than the thin PEEK post or SMT terminal. Define cycle counts, loads and retention/resistance limits before qualification. |
| Installed audio and interference | Complete powered standalone unit with real converter, DSP/control/LED loads, external audio connections and isolated metal base. Capture rail ripple, common-mode coupling, startup/shutdown transients and audio residuals. | Use the actual complete-unit bandwidth, weighting, amplitude and load contract. Driver-only or supply-only results do not establish complete audio performance. EMI methods, numerical limits and environmental applicability require an explicit test specification. |

The [repair status](REPAIRS.md) identifies current design holds. Completing one row does not remove another row's failure or missing limit. No instrument reading, calibration, sample, operator, fabrication approval or successful test is inferred from this document.
