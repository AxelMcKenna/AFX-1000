# Three-board qualification decision

**Qualification is withheld for all three pictured PCB candidates.** Native connectivity checks, independent circuit/layout review and source-bound analyses identify failures and unclosed operating boundaries. No assembled-board measurement has been performed. This packet is an engineering assessment, not a fabrication, power-up or complete-unit release.

See the separate [active repair record](REPAIRS.md) for E12-R2, P03, E28 and their inlet/enclosure integration. The original candidate assessment below remains attached to its original source revisions; it does not describe a new repair as qualified.

The E27 candidate does not silently replace the pictured E26 or its archived routing. A native pass cannot certify a manufacturer's land pattern or an amplifier's analog behavior.

The separate E27 repair reduces the disconnect to **8 opens and 21 native warnings**, with zero ERC/parity findings, 8,860 segments and 518 vias. Its copper resistance diagnostic is superseded: repeated physical lands sharing one logical MOSFET terminal were overwritten in the electrode lookup. Its footprint review also misread TI's 1.100 mm gap dimension as a land width; the source/gate lands are 1.372 × 0.710 mm. See the [explicit corrections](ERRATA.md) before using E27's historical report. E28 is a separate repair with corrected terminal geometry and extraction; its results cannot qualify E26 or E27.

An external, unmodified TI THS4551 model was also run with bundled ngspice 45.2. Six declared AC scenarios are solver-clean; nominal differential gain is about **0.4859418877 V/V at 1 kHz**. Two of twelve total runs retain solver warnings and are rejected. Even cleaner zero-start DC sweeps show an unresolved common-mode discrepancy under the static ground-load hypothesis, so **all DC headroom interpretations remain rejected**. These are conditional simulations of an amplifier stage, without the full servo, reference/pump, actual switching ADC input or extracted board parasitics. They do not qualify the board's clipping, phase margin, stability or audio performance. The vendor model stays external and is not redistributed.

## Quantitative failures and corrections

The legacy digital source spans **4.884113–5.124243 V** under the full-temperature reference envelope and existing ±10 mV excursion allocation. The target is 4.9–5.1 V. A divider would need nominal upper resistance **≥10,550.624 Ω and ≤10,450.722 Ω simultaneously**. Even the conditional 0–85 °C calculation fails the load target using only the earlier 3.5 mΩ switch/star plus four 1 mΩ contacts and **zero wire resistance**: **4.895584–5.099181 V**. The contact values and temperature ceiling are allocations, not measured properties.

The input/preconnected-ramp scenario at a 2.4 A digital reserve demands **3.115738 A**, exceeding the earlier guard's **2.873940 A** allocation. This narrow screen does not include every rail charging event or establish late-closure/SOA behavior. The existing board cannot be qualified by substituting the separate P02 model.

The E12 bias-health upper limit of **−0.16 V** leaves only **0.29388 V** under the inherited modeled output-headroom corner, below the project's 0.30 V reserve. The derived conditional boundary is approximately **−0.16612 V before tolerances**. This rejects the old monitor specification; it does not approve a new threshold or establish physical clipping. Q02 leaves the replacement bias limits undefined, rather than accepting the rejected window.

P02 also contained an incorrect retained-capacitance assumption: the actual disconnect analog bulk is **1,500 µF ±20%**, whose maximum alone is 1.8 mF. Its old 1.1 mF “upper” network value was invalid. The corrected scenario uses **2.2 mF analog / 7.1 mF digital**, with extra capacitance explicitly unverified. Analog initial charging demand is now **2.805 A** and stored-energy scenario **27.665 mJ**. Complete-network upper bounds are unestablished; the correction is not a startup or stability approval.

## Measurements still required

The [Q02 source-bound capture plan](../../qualification/boards/README.md) targets the exact three pictured variants. It separates unpowered inspection, signed DC corner tests, ADC supply/headroom/stability/leakage and transfer behavior, disconnect fault/late-closure/residual-charge behavior, and thermal/EMI characterization. It uses appropriate instrument roles, current calibrations, reviewed fixture limits, real as-built/fabrication evidence, raw captures and a documented expanded-uncertainty decision rule. Unspecified operating limits and absolute stress limits cannot produce operating qualification.

Testing requires manufactured/populated reviewed variants; selected stackup, component MPNs and harness/return interfaces; independently reviewed source/load/fault fixtures; and suitable DMM, oscilloscope/differential/current probes, electronic loads, audio/FRA/spectrum and temperature/leakage equipment. No destructive fault settings or full-unit power-up are authorized by this packet. The missing equipment/board status has been requested from the owner.

Original boards and historical review archives remain preserved. New repairs, diagnostic models and fixture records are separate variants. Source hashes bind the evidence to its actual snapshot. A later layout/source change requires renewed review; no software checker can authenticate that a physical measurement or board identity is truthful.
