# Isolated AUX schematic candidate 01

This is an editable, native-checked candidate of the selected 3.3 V AUX architecture. It is **not promoted to TBP72**, contains no PCB, and is not fabrication-ready. `authored/power_protection.kicad_sch` is the project entry; `candidate-bom.csv` is the native exported population, including inherited missing supplier fields.

The candidate adds TPS62175DQCR, its 10 µH inductor, input/output capacitors and 105 kΩ / 20 kΩ divider; adds TPS3703A5330DSER, its 10 kΩ reset pullup and 100 nF bypass; applies eight resistor corrections and changes P_C109 to 680 pF. P_U102 pins 8/5 and its P_C102 bypass move to the independent 5 V preregulator output. E_U31 pin 6 receives the fast reset instead of AUX. VIN_IC overvoltage sensing stays upstream. Existing E_U19/E_U35, their pins and all other baseline connectivity remain intact. The legacy net name PWR_V2V5_AUX is deliberately retained to avoid unrelated renaming; its intended voltage in this candidate is 3.298633 V.

## Selected passives and receiving limits

P_L900 is **Coilcraft XAL4040-103MEC**, 10 µH ±20%, DCR ≤92.4 mΩ at 25°C, 3 A at a **typical** 30% inductance reduction. This offers substantial room above the buck's 1.2 A maximum current-limit point. It does not prove inductance retention over current, tolerance and temperature; 3 A is not a guaranteed no-saturation limit. The primary Coilcraft PDF is retained under `primary`.

P_C900 is **TDK C3216X7R1H225K160AB**, 2.2 µF ±10%, 50 V X7R, 1206. P_C901 is **TDK C3225X7R1C226K250AC**, 22 µF ±10%, 16 V X7R, 1210. The latter is NRND, so this is a prototype selection, not a long-term procurement approval. Nominal capacitance/voltage are verified on the manufacturer pages linked in each BOM entry. Neither selection establishes the allocated effective-capacitance limits: initial tolerance plus the full ±15% temperature allowance alone allows 1.683 µF / 16.83 µF before DC bias and aging, below the previous 1.76 µF / 17.6 µF receiving allocations. Therefore **these LC receiving limits remain unmet, and this candidate must not be promoted on nominal capacitance alone**. TDK PDF download was HTTP 403; the linked manufacturer specification pages were readable.

TI SLVA543 (retained primary PDF/text) describes typical filter characterization using 50% capacitor derating, at 3.3 V output and 7.2/12/24 V inputs. That supports a practical stability experiment; it is not a guaranteed 5 V/full-temperature stability envelope. Verify effective capacitance at actual voltage/temperature/aging and the implemented filter's load-step response before accepting or selecting a final capacitor array. Do not equate the datasheet's typical ±20% variation assumption with a manufacturer component guarantee.

P_R900/P_R901 are **TNPW0805105KBEEA / TNPW080520K0BEEA** (0.1%, 25 ppm/K); E_R900 is **TNPW080510K0BEEA**; P_R108 becomes **TNPW080517K6BEEA**. Vishay 28758 supports E192, the resistance range, B tolerance, E TCR and EA packaging. P_C109 is **TDK C1608C0G1H681J080AA**, 680 pF ±5%, 50 V C0G; its schematic footprint changes from 0805 to the actual 0603 package, requiring future PCB replacement. Four 0.01% / 5 ppm E-divider resistors retain explicit requirements; their supplier fields are not invented. P_R117/P_R118/P_R119 select Vishay TNPW0805392KBYEA / TNPW080518K0BYEA / TNPW080511K7BYEA (0.1%, 10 ppm/K). The two new ICs and inductor have no assigned footprint until package land-pattern review. This is explicit in `verification.json`.

## Functional review and remaining qualification

TPS62175 cold start is documented: SLVSB35C §8.4.4 states sleep mode is not entered until soft start completes; §8.4.1 ends soft start at regulation. SLEEP tied to VOUT is explicitly recommended. The conditional 4.8707 V output minimum exceeds the 0.9 V SLEEP high threshold. The 10 mV/µs startup slope is typical, not a maximum startup delay. Pin 11 is the candidate exposed-pad number; source pin functions require the exposed pad tied to both ground domains, which remain one net here.

The TPS3703 RESET drives only E_U31 pin 6 through the new 10 kΩ pullup. At steady AUX 3.2196–3.3782 V, AUP2G08 requires VIH ≥2.0 V and VIL ≤0.9 V. The supervisor VOL table gives 0.25 V at discrete VDD/test currents; its 30 µs assertion maximum requires 5% overdrive and 10 pF loading. Full actual-load/voltage coverage is not inferred from typical curves. The 5% overdrive endpoint is 2.9574 V, below the logic's 3.0–3.6 V timing band and above its separate 2.3–2.7 V band. **The earlier 1 µs logic-delay allocation is not a guarantee through this gap.** Keep decisive gates in a tabulated supply band or qualify a replacement before shutdown closure.

The corrected input-OV filter has nominal τ = 4.8218 µs. With 0.1% / 10 ppm resistors over a 100°C delta (±0.2% combined) and 680 pF C0G ±5% plus 30 ppm/°C over 100°C (±5.3% conservative sum), τ is 4.5571–5.0875 µs. This bounds a passive time constant, not comparator detection time or safe overvoltage duration. No product fault-withstand limit was supplied to convert it into a qualification pass.

Startup sequencing maxima, comparator error coverage, complete cross-domain transitions, whole-system current, mounted thermal resistance, actual paired MOSFET gate discharge and hard local AUX shorts remain separate acceptance items. A 100 mA allocation is not a proven application or fault-current bound. Existing source-bound calculations live in `../fast_inhibit`; do not treat their sensitivity results or native ERC as electrical qualification.

## Self-review disposition

Antagonistic self-review found and repaired the initial off-grid pins, the missing output power-source declaration, stale P_R108 supplier value, and missing native-input provenance. Native runs now hash their exact inputs before export; the verifier rejects drift. A second check confirmed P_C109's selected package is 0603 and explicitly records its footprint change. No new ERC exclusion was added.

Unresolved promotion blockers are the LC effective-value counterexample, the 2.7–3.0 V logic timing gap, three unreviewed new footprints, four unspecified precision-divider supplier parts, incomplete operating-current/thermal/transient bounds, and startup/actual-MOSFET discharge qualification. These are engineering limits of the candidate, not failures hidden by the native check. The full existing numerical suite passes 25 tests; the native exhaustive pin/net comparison is an additional check. Parent integration owns the PR and any subsequent PCB changes.
