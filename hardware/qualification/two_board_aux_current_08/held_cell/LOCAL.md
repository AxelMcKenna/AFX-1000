# Local held reservoir/LDO/supervisor copper

`native-local-02` completes all 18 local connections with 35 B.Cu tracks and eight ordinary 0.6/0.3 mm ground vias. Candidate opens fall 1964 → 1946, exactly the declared native joins. No original pad, track, via, component pose or net assignment changes. All 48 complete zone objects and fill geometry are retained; native save reordered zone records, so verification compares exact objects by unique UUID rather than relying on record order. All 55 formerly anonymous pads retain unique full physical identity.

Actual native added-copper polygons have zero uncovered reference area on B.Cu against the existing main In6 fill plus the verified extension. All eight ground vias contact actual main filled copper. No protected quiet object or plane outline is contacted. Final DRC contains exactly the four pre-existing, unwaived DSE errors and no added warnings/errors. The selected power PCB remains unchanged.

The routed cell includes the 4.7 Ω feed resistor, both 220 µF reservoir capacitors, TPS7A2033 input/enable, 100 nF input bypass, 2.2 µF output capacitor, minimum-load bleeder, 5 V supervisor supply/100 nF bypass and local SENSE connection. Supervisor RESET, the upstream 5 V/VIN feeds and the wider held-rail distribution remain open and require later routing. This is not a complete powered circuit or fabrication release.

Routing resolves fine-pitch endpoints inside clearance-valid pad copper. Ground escapes are routed before wide power traces: the initial greedy input/enable routes otherwise trapped the LDO ground in a clearance pocket. The first native candidate's eight off-center via-endpoint warnings were rejected; the final paths end exactly at via centers. Compact evidence is in `rejected-local-endpoints.json`; prior screen reports retain the identified failures.

`verify_local.py` validates exact intended source/target pairs and pin nets, actual native copper/reference geometry, source hashes, complete retained zones, exact native group joins and confined library hashes. Five tests reject missing links, wrong GND/RESET assignments and off-center returns. `local-copper.png` is a visually inspected source-bound rendering of actual B.Cu copper in board-coordinate projection; it is not a component-body or bottom-view drawing.

`trace-screen.json` uses actual saved centerline lengths. At assumed 35 µm finished copper and 125 °C, with the receiving current of 100 mA flowing through every listed path, trace-only drops are approximately 0.831 mV from R902 output to LDO input, 0.130 mV from LDO output to C904, and 0.253 mV from the LDO ground pad to its via. These exclude pad copper, via barrels, plane spreading, capacitor ESR and external distribution; they do not qualify the 20 mV ESR/local-drop allocation. The 100 mA drain, 200 µF effective reservoir, 1 µs logic allocation, 81.00348 µs modeled hold interval and 50.00348 µs driver turnoff allocation remain conditional.

Libraries are linked to the unchanged source inside the same worktree. The native run uses an absolute read-only source link inside Docker, then replaces it with the verified portable relative link for handoff. The process adapter preserves native diagnostics and refuses to treat a nonzero/timeout result as success.
