# Higher-headroom standalone bench handoff

This is an executable **measurement plan**, not measured evidence or a power-up release. It applies to the higher-headroom external-converter design, its E14/E25 baseline interfaces and F05 storage firmware. B1, the onboard-codec wiring and the old A0 ±24 V commissioning procedure are different designs. P01 is a separate proposed source redesign; it cannot be populated on the existing PSU PCB or tested under this baseline plan without a new review.

No physical tests have been performed by these tools. The generated capture template contains no readings, timestamps, instrument identities or fabricated waveform files. `verify` records the parser checks and the empty-template result under `hardware/validation/bench-plan/`; its physical-test count stays zero. Instrument access, actual assembled boards and a printer have not been confirmed.

```sh
python3 -m hardware.qualification.generate
python3 -m hardware.qualification.check hardware/qualification/generated/capture-template.json
python3 -m hardware.qualification.verify
```

Copy the template into the actual experiment's capture directory before entering measurements. Keep raw scope/logic/audio/temperature logs, photographs, firmware image, fixture review and calibration certificates there. Run the checker with `--captures-root /path/to/captures`; `--output result.json` writes the derived case states. Add `--require-pass` to exit2 unless every physical case comparison passes. Without it, exit0 means the record structure/comparisons were evaluated successfully, even when its states are unrun/blocked/failed; it is not a qualification pass. Generation fingerprints current source files and records an informational `source_base_commit`. A later commit alone does not stale unchanged inputs: the checker reconstructs the specification using the recorded base context and exact current source hashes. An unmeasured template also remains checkable in a shallow clone that lacks the informational base Git object; the result explicitly marks that historical context unverified. A modified source, edited plan, changed limit or changed hard hold requires regeneration and review, rather than changing only the capture JSON.

## Stages and existing holds

1. Review the isolated-board fixture, source/current/fuse limits, polarity, component ratings, netlists and test connections. Measure E23 alone into dummy loads. Do not attach the whole unit at this stage.
2. Measure each source rail under no/half/maximum allocated load, with local returns, shared-return loading and temperature recorded. Do not interpret a source-rail pass as a protected-load or outgoing-harness pass.
3. After complete-system design review and assembly release, measure all protected stars, load/ripple response, all-rail startup, shutdown, brownout and late gate closure. Use an independently reviewed energy-limited fault fixture for any injection; this plan does not prescribe an unreviewed fault voltage or fault current.
4. Capture every gate/common-source bank, physical relay contact, manual bypass and gain transition. Stop the real audio ISR and foreground separately, freeze heartbeat/permit independently, and repeat faults over approved phase/temperature/supply conditions. Latch-Q feedback does not prove actual relay contact state.
5. Walk all controls, indicators, touch/angle interfaces and sensor/bus faults. Test real-card missing/malformed/removal/slow-read and allocation-refusal paths, SDMMC cache/DMA/guard behavior, card inrush and callback timing. Measure filesystem/allocation counts around the actual audio callback.
6. Calibrate and measure the entire stereo analog chain: gain, mode transitions, frequency response, noise/distortion, headroom/impedance, round-trip latency and reference effect behavior.
7. Characterize enclosed thermal and EMI behavior, then run the two-hour standalone audio/control session and repeat faults warm. A two-hour standalone test does not establish rekordbox routing behavior.

Current hard holds include incomplete E25 routing; no implemented P01 schematic/layout; downstream load-harness/fanout budgets; the large connected-capacitance/loop/inrush question; unselected peripheral/anode/fanout assemblies; and remaining unrouted/unqualified boards. The generated plan reads current local evidence for the available E25 open count and interface gaps. It preserves an explicit full-unit power-up hold even when source model calculations pass. It does not authorize physically testing the partially routed E25 candidate.

P01's conditional DC envelope ends at the E25 protected output star **before outgoing load contacts/fanouts**. Its report records the TPS62903 direct-output capacitance recommendation versus the connected millifarad network as unresolved. Passing an allocated DC calculation does not close those concerns. Existing source, F05 and E25 host/native records are prerequisites and context, not substitutes for physical measurements.

## Values and limits

Signed normal rail targets come from `power_supply/integration.json`: +19 V is 18.81–19.19 V; −19 V is −19.19..−18.81 V; ±12 V has the corresponding 11.88–12.12 V magnitude; analog 5 V is 4.975–5.025 V; digital 5 V is 4.9–5.1 V. Load allocations come from E17's `normal_drop` screen. Measure negative rail voltages with their signs intact. The source-rail target comparison is a necessary screen; the precision/current model and outgoing distribution still require their separate checks.

The digital 10 mV ripple excursion and the protection peak-voltage screens are **design allocations**. The old 125 µs interruption term is not a guaranteed gate or fault-to-dry time. Full fault interruption, startup/ramp/inrush, analog ripple/load-step/settling, contact bounce/click, complete scan latency, SD hard startup time, thermal and EMI limits remain unqualified. The checker records such measurements but derives `blocked` until acceptance requirements are established in the reviewed specification. It never turns a typical datasheet curve or a host-test result into an acceptance guarantee.

The F05 loader has an 8-second **inter-operation** budget. A blocking SDK call can overrun it; the pinned SD driver uses 30-second polling limits. This plan therefore deliberately has no hard 8-second boot acceptance limit. The callback's 2400 µs value is the existing firmware trip threshold, not a measured end-to-end deadline guarantee.

Audio project targets are taken from `docs/specifications.md` and the retained commissioning requirements: SNR ≥98 dB MASTER / ≥102 dB SEND-RETURN, THD ≤0.005%, headroom ≥20 dB, input impedance ≥22 kΩ and output impedance ≤1 kΩ into the declared 10 kΩ load. Record source/analyzer residuals, bandwidth, weighting, reference level, load and clipping criterion. Frequency-response tolerance and the final comparison method remain open; a simple number does not prove a reference hardware match, and THD+N must not silently replace THD.

## Capture format and derived states

Each case has one reading object per required condition, with every named metric present. Units and limits are in the plan. Use numeric scalars and actual booleans, never formatted strings or `NaN`/infinity. The record identifies the assembly, each case's DUT board/revision, operator, actual firmware source-commit context and complete source hash map, actual firmware image/hash, source/current/fuse/load/return setup, ambient temperature and a fixture approval bound to this plan. A case may override the global fixture release when its isolated-board or system scope differs. The device source commit may be a later descendant of the plan base; do not label a newly built commit as the old base. Physical case comparisons remain blocked when that ancestry cannot be established from available history (for example in a shallow clone); fetch the needed history before claiming a measurement pass. An explicit operator attestation binds the image to the declared source snapshot. The checker verifies image integrity and commit context, but does not independently rebuild the binary or prove which image is flashed.

Every used instrument has a unique ID, kind/model/serial and a calibration interval/certificate covering the whole measurement interval. The initial schema requires current calibration records for all listed instrument kinds; a non-calibratable trace/debug tool needs a reviewed method/schema extension rather than a fabricated certificate. Raw captures must cover all required condition labels and have nonempty, relative paths and matching SHA-256 values. Paths and symlinks cannot escape the capture directory. Capture start/end times must be ordered, in UTC and not in the future. A claimed soak duration cannot exceed its timestamp interval.

States are `planned`, `not_run`, `blocked`, `failed` and `measured_pass`. Empty planned/not-run records make no physical claim. A blocked entry needs a reason. Attempted measurements require their full metadata and raw evidence even if a criterion failed. The checker derives failure from violated known criteria, derives blocked from unresolved criteria/system holds, and rejects a claimed pass that disagrees. Partial/missing readings, missing calibration, stale sources, missing or altered files and mismatched firmware are errors. A passing isolated-board case does not release a system stage. System cases also depend on every earlier-stage case comparison; missing/unrun/blocked/failed earlier stages prevent a later measured_pass. Isolated source-board tests remain independently scoped to their reviewed fixtures.

The checker verifies structure, source binding, artifact integrity and comparisons. **It cannot authenticate whether a human actually made a measurement or whether an unparsed waveform supports the entered numbers.** Raw-capture interpretation and review remain required; no result grants full-unit power-up, fabrication, compliance or build readiness. Synthetic parser fixtures exist only in temporary unit-test directories, are explicitly labeled and cannot be accepted by the production CLI as physical bench evidence.
