# NF05H — independent absolute-AUX cutoff proposal, 261 parts

This is a NEW private schematic successor to the frozen 249-part standalone sequencer. It is reviewable native circuitry, **not** a routed board or an accepted startup/protection implementation. All parent seals, existing NF/E28 PCBs and mechanical sources remain unchanged.

An independent RAW5-powered TPS389001 supervises AUX. Its open-drain RESET directly controls a TPS22919 switch supplying the two series clamp-LED strings. Neither AUX-powered HCS logic nor an assumed intermediate-power output state can override that energy switch. A 240 Ω discharge resistor and explicit complete positive-leakage/capacitance requirements replace an unsupported reliance on the switch's typical QOD/tOFF. QOD is physically NC. Under the stated divider/ref/bias conditions, falling trip is 2.420522–2.490211 V and rising release 2.428392–2.510746 V. R92/R94 use TNPU080511K3HWEN00 / TNPU080510K0HWEN00 (.02%/2 ppm) with a complete **0.35% receiving** requirement; the small 49.9 Ω TNPW trim separately requires **0.7%**. These are conditional requirements, not manufacturer guarantees over an unspecified service life. The independent passive audit’s 8000 h separate-test sums are useful comparisons; 225000 h does not satisfy these complete allocations and no service-life target is adopted. An AUX-OV hardgate is not implemented.

The actual native schematic/export has 261 components, zero ERC errors, two exact unchanged isolated-secondary GND1 warnings (U19/U24) and exact declared native pin/value parity. Seven supervisor maps are checked independently against TI's original physical pin table, not merely against the builder. **TPS3890 VDD is pin 4 and RESET is pin 6.** The frozen 249 checkpoint's TWO new START timers U52/U54 had these swapped. Its FOUR inherited floating monitors were already correct. `errata.json` and `generated/equivalence.json` retain the precise scope. The old checkpoint is rejected for those new timer pin maps; no old hashes are replaced.

A second real defect was the old RAW5-powered LVC1G06 sink's 0.7×VCC input HIGH requirement, which AUX control could not guarantee. U4/U10 and their actual local bypasses C3/C12 now use AUX; the open-drain outputs remain 5.5 V tolerant. R4/R11 become 402 Ω, keeping the full sink load below the 8 mA/2.3 V VOL test point. The conditional LED IF minimum is about 3.77 mA with both maximum forward drops, maximum switch drop and 0.3 V sink drop included. Hot LED voltage, trigger/return current, lifetime, propagation and output resistance are still receiving conditions; 25°C photo-relay data is not a full-temperature guarantee.

The AUP2G14 VCC−0.1 V row applies at **20 µA**, not 100 µA. R3/R10 are therefore 205 kΩ. With the declared 1 µA each receiver/board/monitor limits, their maximum source load is about 16.58 µA, or 19.89 µA with 20% planning margin. A disconnected-source comparison using 0.6 µA AUP Ioff plus 1 µA receiver and 1 µA board leakage gives 0.6588 V with 20% margin, below the receiver's 0.7 V LOW limit. That comparison is not extrapolated into undefined intermediate-AUX states. Monitor pins must not be externally driven.

`generated/analysis.json` preserves the loaded cutoff constraints: complete positive cathode/output/board-source leakage 11.2 µA, all-node stored capacitance ≤0.22 µF, explicit finite paths behind LED shunts, hot closed-contact RON ≤20 Ω, active controller/bank source current ≤1 mA and complete gate charge ≤1 µC are **unaccepted receiving requirements**. Under them the closed gate equilibrium is 0.2466 V and discharge to a 0.3 V target is finite. A MOSFET threshold voltage is not used as proof of acceptable drain OFF current. Si8751 source-current bounds, actual relay closing, loaded pass-FET SOA, fault current/energy, RAW5 collapse/reverse current and hot leakage must be qualified. A 1 kΩ bleed fails the conservative no-forward-voltage-credit OFF-current screen and is preserved as a rejected comparison.

Recovery is a material design hold: the UV supervisor automatically restores LED energy after recovery. If GLOBAL/PERMIT/COMPLETE remained asserted, that can produce late bank reclosure. This proposal does **not** fake a physical GLOBAL-OFF interval or reset fault latches from SAFE/UV to hide that problem. The real minimum OFF interval, fault-latched UV recovery and asynchronous reset priority must be implemented/verified before adoption. The two LED MID nodes currently have no individual resistive discharge paths: a pair-wide shunt does not prove their junction-charge decay. The RC-path estimate is a diagnostic, not a distributed LED-current or loaded-cutoff upper bound. Separate per-LED shunts are a concrete next repair to evaluate; actual hot optical response still requires characterization. Normal START timing does not bound that fault case. The module's ≥22.8 V settled output before first ARM remains an unimplemented receiving correlation; its 25.2 V maximum is required even during no-load/startup/transients.

J6 separately supplies guarded 12 V and `MODULE_INPUT_GND` to the two TMR primaries/input capacitors; those five electrodes are not tied to quiet AGND on this board. J1.4 is now physical NC. J1.2 still carries the RAW5 LED/bleed and AUX return currents; it is not an ideal quiet star and the separate J6 return does not remove that shared impedance. The normal primary-current screen is 0.17413 A at 9 V input; 20 µF is only the initial nominal input-cap subtotal, with inrush/hot effective C unbounded. Secondary 804 Ω feed resistance is not used to dismiss primary connector loss. The keyed XH2 candidate, mates, wiring, contacts, common-source return coupling and strain support remain unaccepted. Original force/clamp headers are unselected; their old 1 mm bores cannot receive the proposed 18 AWG pigtails. A new soldered force interface and its additional wire resistance still require implementation.

The old NF AUX allocation 2.548–2.652 V was not enclosed by actual P03 source corners 2.533479–2.656441 V, even before harness loss. The NEW local receiving proposal is 2.52–2.70 V: it permits only 13.479 mV downward source/distribution/reference loss and leaves 9.254 mV above the worst UV release threshold. Shared RAW5/AUX return loss, coupled source offsets, ripple and uncertainty all consume that allowance; no zero-ground assumption is made. Source-path acceptance remains false.

The explicit DIG receiving proposal is **2.44 A**. Known guarded partitions total about 2.43713 A; AUX provisions are 10 mA normal / 13 mA nonrail fault **inside** the existing 90 mA source pool. The AUX ledger also includes a separate 0.4 mA DC output/feedback provision derived from actual OPA2333 R23/R24/R40/R41 paths, rather than counting its quiescent current alone. Capacitor charging, invalid rail overvoltage and internal-short peaks are not bounded by that DC provision. That pool partition, source voltage/ground matrix, regulator heating and complete hot current remain unaccepted. The +20 mA planning delta uses 5.1 V and 85% efficiency (+0.12 W), giving a provisional capacity heat screen 37.27034 W. The separate normal 11.4 V/0.36 Ω feed point is about 2.83885 A / 10.37802 V. Normal feed I²R is not added a second time to the capacity corner. Fan stall/standby and complete fault heat are not bounded here. Neither the normal-current calculation nor the common 12 V guard qualifies a short on the small raw5/control or separate module-input harness; branch fault-current protection remains unimplemented/unaccepted.

`generated/footprint-area.json` inventories actual captured courtyards: approximately 4487 mm²; 1.2×sum≈5384 mm² exceeds the 5075 mm² single-face 2 mm-inset area of a 149×39 board. This is an area-only warning, not a packing impossibility: actual backside allocation and local critical loops must be solved. No old 231/233-part placement, airflow or routing is inherited. Six DCU and seven DCK6 pattern references use actual TI lands. The isolated two-footprint native fixture has zero DRC/opens; enlarging one DCU pad produces the required real clearance failure. That fixture is **not** an NF05H PCB or fabricator approval. Exact passive parts and manufacturer lands/aging/effective-C requirements are still under independent audit.

`generated/N12-retune-comparison.json` separately evaluates the frozen P03 source and an E28 42.8295 mΩ copper diagnostic, never a certified upper bound. A 180680 Ω R40 total balances conditional low/high margins near 13.72/14.21 mV with 0.154 A, retained dynamic/return allocations and 1.2×DC drop. No source resistor, complete lifetime tolerance, copper/contact or endpoint acceptance is adopted.
