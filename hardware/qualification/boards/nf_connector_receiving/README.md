# NC01 connector preselection and local harness study

This is a source-bound **249-part NF05S preselection snapshot**, not a replacement PCB or a full-unit connector fit. FR01 remains immutable. The later unsealed 261-part absolute-AUX successor and proposed split J6 module-primary return have not been silently substituted. Final connector pin contracts and board placement must follow that electrical owner's next stable source.

The compact control-header candidate is **JST B30B-PHDSS / PHDR-30VS / SPHD-002T-P0.5**, with Alpha3049 AWG26 as a wire candidate. The public manufacturer drawings establish nominal geometry and reference assembly dimensions; maximum installed dimensions, retention, dry-circuit reliability and crimp settings remain open. PHD is friction-lock, not a positive latch. It is not a drop-in replacement for the old rotated 2.54 mm header.

The public [current PHD catalog](https://www.jst-mfg.com/product/pdf/eng/ePHD.pdf) gives 2 mm pitch, 28 mm contact span and nominal31.9 ×5 mm body for the30-circuit header; reference mated height8.8 mm. Its 3 A rating is specifically at AWG22 and is not credited to this AWG26 proposal. Contact resistance is10 mΩ initial /20 mΩ after the listed environmental tests. The temperature range is−25..85°C including current rise. Actual current, installed temperature and minimum dry-circuit application suitability still need validation.

The copied newer four-page public regional catalog labels its hole diagram as **mounting-face view**; the older two-page regional catalog says **soldering-side view**. They are preserved separately. The actual captured KiCad PHD library has pad1[0,0],pad2[0,2],pad3[2,0] and pad30[28,2]; all30 are independently checked. Existing generic NF J1 at90° has its even pin row in the opposite board direction. Preserve number→net identity, deliberately rebuild the key orientation/pin map and reroute; do not copy pin locations visually from an old view.

Public PHD catalog pin pitch tolerance is nonaccumulating±0.05 mm and its reference drill is0.7..0.8 mm. The copied library's0.75 mm drills and1.2 mm lands are parsed data, not a factory-approved land/drill construction. Catalog board thickness1.6 mm does not establish acceptance of a1.76 mm maximum stack. Exact JST2D/CAD/manual links require a company/personal-information form and restrictive document terms. No terms were accepted, no data submitted and no restricted documents redistributed. Public catalog PDFs are in `sources/primary/`.

Alpha3049 has OD1.2446..1.3462 mm and a10D bend rule, giving minimum centre radius13.462 mm. Alpha3050 is another OD-compatible option with R14.732 mm. **Alpha3051 is rejected**: its maximum OD1.6256 exceeds the PHD1.5 mm window. These facts come from [Alpha3049](https://www.alphawire.com/en/products/wire/hook-up-wire/premium/3049), [Alpha3050](https://www.alphawire.com/en/products/wire/hook-up-wire/premium/3050) and the captured customer specifications; DCR values are nominal, not guaranteed hot limits. Wire current/temperature derating, AWM style on received stock and actual crimp compatibility remain unaccepted.

`generated/local-PHD30-fanout.json` gives30 real local reference centre-lines in connector coordinates: R14 arcs, with the lower row departing10 mm farther before turning upward. Identical unstaggered row arcs approach each other and fail the mutation test. All435 pair combinations are certified continuously using interval bounds, with maximum OD,0.24 mm intrinsic relative-position stress,0.25 clearance,0.005 reserve per wire and a provable chord cover. Straight/arc joins have continuous tangent under arc-length parameterization; curvature jumps from0 to1/14. This is a local exit model only: the actual wire exit plane/crimp alignment, continuous connections to remote endpoints, retention and whole-unit collision checks remain open. The NF261 successor's new J6 supply means its TMR primary bursts must not be inferred to traverse this old30-pin map.

## Force and clamp connectors

The [public PH catalog](https://www.jst-mfg.com/product/pdf/eng/ePH.pdf) supports **B2B-PH-K-S / PHR-2 / SPH-002T-P0.5S** as a keyed/shrouded low-current J3/J5 clamp candidate, with8 mm reference mated height. It does not close the force-path allowance. PHD starts at8 circuits; a two-circuit B2B-PHDSS is not an available catalog part.

J2/J4's existing5 mΩ total new-force-contact allowance is an unaccepted allocation. Two PH/PHD series mating interfaces total20 mΩ initial /40 mΩ after the listed tests, **4×/8× the allocation before any wire or additional endpoints**. A higher-current label does not solve this. [Molex Mega-Fit product specification1720631000-PS RevD](https://www.molex.com/content/dam/molex/molex-dot-com/products/automated/en-us/productspecificationpdf/172/172063/1720631000-PS-000.pdf) gives2 mΩ initial per interface, but listed temperature-life/shock changes can add2 mΩ per interface: two interfaces may reach8 mΩ, excluding wire. No specific Mega-Fit system is selected here and no lifetime5 mΩ claim is made.

The concrete alternate is the new **direct-solder force footprint proposal**, two3.4 mm lands /2.1 mm bores /5 mm pitch. Native KiCad10.0.7 successfully parsed it and the actual PHD30 footprint:32 pads total. This is a parser check, **not PCB DRC or fit**. The old1 mm force holes are smaller than Alpha6715's nominal1.2192 mm18AWG conductor. With a proposed bare/tinned bundle≤1.4 mm, relative centring0.3 and geometry reserve0.005, the2.1 mm bore leaves only0.045 mm idealized radial gauge margin; received wire, stripping, hole/finish and solder-process acceptance are still required. The old2.54 mm pitch and pose cannot be inherited.

Four solder joints at≤1 mΩ each are a **measurement allocation**, not a claimed property of solder. They could fit the5 mΩ contact allowance, but wire resistance must be added separately. `force-pigtail-proposal.json` includes two-lead length/hot-DCR sensitivity and its effect on the explicitly conditional old source-recentre comparisons. It does not turn nominal DCR or an unimplemented source change into an endpoint pass. A separate insulating clamp/support must take the pull load before the first bend; its geometry, material, fasteners, compression, creep and pull acceptance are **not designed or qualified yet**. Solder joints are not strain anchors. The proposed force footprint is not ready for board adoption until that mechanical design and final pin contract are coherent.
