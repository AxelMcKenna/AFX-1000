# AX02 — integrated POWER_RUN leaf receiving successor

This private **schematic-only** successor preserves AX01's 78-file evidence packet and P03 `db83`. It replaces AX01's discrete NMOS / back-to-back PMOS / clamp-diode cell with a TPS22913CYZVR voltage-based reverse-protection load switch. It requires no new regulator or RAW5 supply and removes the common-source MOSFET bootstrap dependency. It does not establish complete shutdown, source-collapse, hot leakage or current qualification.

The actual ten-part native bench schematic contains eight proposed integrated additions plus two unselected bench headers. TPS389001DSET still senses the original internal POWER_RUN at its fixed 1.15 V threshold. Internal R113 remains 3.3 kΩ, U102 PG remains physical pin 3 and U102 EN remains pin 5 tied to VIN_IC. Only external J106.3 would be disconnected from that internal node and fed by the new leaf through the existing FH contact to E28 J5.3. The new switch's physical pins are A1=OUT, A2=VIN, B1=GND, B2=ON; they match the actual exported netlist. There are no discrete MOSFETs or diodes in AX02.

ON has an explicit 100 kΩ pullup to AUX and 1 MΩ pulldown to AGND. It does not rely on any typical internal pulldown. Under the stated leakage/resistor/source receiving conditions, ON HIGH is 2.164581 V versus the 1.1 V primary input requirement (1.32 V target after ×1.2). TPS3890 LOW is at most 0.25 V under its applicable 0.4 mA table condition, below the switch's 0.4 V VIL; ×1.2 is 0.30 V. The 0.8 V POR/15 µA loaded-output test also has numerical current margin with the weak external pullup. Leakage at every intermediate or unpowered input voltage remains a receiving requirement; the switch's 1 µA ON-leak row specifies only 1.4–5.25 V or GND. The threshold calculation does not prove all partial-power transitions.

A 100 Ω 1206 resistor limits the external leaf. With 0.2 Ω switch RON as a continuous-condition receiving enclosure, the retained 250 Ω complete FH/contact/wire loop, E28's actual R93 and input leakage allocation, settled HIGH is 2.436972 V (36.972 mV above the 2.4 V target). The primary's full-temperature 0.12 Ω row is at VIN=2.5 V / 200 mA, so the actual light-load / continuous VIN enclosure is not silently claimed as a table guarantee. The 10 µA complete OFF-path requirement remains unaccepted; supply current at VOUT=0 is not a separately specified hot leakage bound for every floating output. Open-leaf LOW ×1.2 remains 0.39552 V under the retained complete leakage scenario.

The native 10 µF input capacitor uses the real part already selected on P03, C3225X7R1E106K250AC. Its effective capacitance must be ≥1 µF at the actual bias/temperature/age/process conditions; this has not been established by its nominal marking. The switch datasheet recommends local input capacitance and approximately 10:1 input-to-output capacitance review for droop. A separate 100 nF bypass remains at the supervisor, and its 10 nF C0G timing capacitor is separate. The known positive initial/temperature input inventory is 12.7765 µF, charge 34.49655 µC at 2.7 V. It excludes unknown internal, cable and output capacitance. At the modeled startup planning margin that charge alone needs approximately 9 ms average ramp; it does not bound peak current, total AUX capacitance, source soft-start, regulator stability or output slew.

The primary switch IQ rows use ON=VIN, while this circuit's divided ON voltage is lower. An additional **1 mA nonrail/switching receiving provision** is included explicitly rather than transferring a microamp rail-input row. That provision is not a manufacturer guarantee. The practical source partition retains the full NF 11/14 mA reserves, AX01's actual E28 state ledger and the unchanged 90 mA AUX planning pool:

| Conditional practical source provision | Guarded current |
|---|---:|
| Normal settled | 82.097 mA |
| Positive startup | 85.402 mA |
| Settled internal RUN-low fault | 50.082 mA |
| Leaf short before E28 settles | 115.151 mA — not closed |
| Uncorrelated transition overlap | 131.036 mA — not closed |

The inherited reference-divider overlap stays a separately identified unused reserve; it is not spent to fit the pool. The TPS7A1601's 100 mA rating is not a guaranteed fault-current clamp. The failed transition columns cannot be promoted to acceptable source voltage or safe fault behavior without actual timing/current/thermal bounds. There is no pool increase and no accepted operating E28 60 mA bound.

TPS22913 is preferable to the rejected TPS22916 comparison because its reverse detector monitors voltage rather than requiring the leaf to reach a typical −500 mA trigger. However, TPS22913's 44 mV reverse threshold, 10 µs response and 0.3 µA after-event reverse leakage are **typical** (leakage at 25°C). Their maximums remain null. Its UVLO table gives 1.2 V rising maximum / 0.5 V falling minimum at the stated VON=0 test; that test cannot be silently treated as a universal active-HIGH 13C partial-power proof. Recommended operation starts at 1.4 V. Stored charge, independently retained host AUX, reverse-event energy, below-range ON/OUT behavior and loaded cutoff are still unqualified. QOD has only a 3.3 V/25°C resistance maximum; there is no inherited 2.5 V hot discharge ceiling.

The YZV mechanical drawing was visually inspected and archived as `sources/TI-YZV-page36-reviewed.png`: maximum body 0.918 ×0.918 mm, seating-plane height 0.50 mm, ball pitch 0.50 mm, physical ball diameter 0.20–0.25 mm. The assigned custom footprint uses a 0.23 mm NSMD receiving land and maximum-body Fab/courtyard envelope. This is an explicit assembly/fabrication proposal, **not** a manufacturer land-pattern approval or C50 process acceptance. No PCB placement, routing or new MCAD fit is implemented.

Nine tests check actual native pin roles, independent enable resistors, original PG loading, full NF reserves, failed transient provisions, capacitor inventory, and refusal to convert typical reverse/timing rows into guaranteed maximums. Native schematic ERC and pin/value parity are zero. All physical, current, shutdown, reverse, partial-power, fabrication and adoption flags remain false. `integration-proposal.json` is a concrete reviewable diff for later P03 implementation, not an edit to the frozen board.
