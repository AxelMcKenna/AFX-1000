# NF02 primary evidence and applicability

This is a pre-native engineering study. Manufacturer electrical limits below keep their original test conditions. `sources/primary-sources.json` distinguishes original downloaded bytes from original PDFs read through the web parser: ADI local downloads timed out, so those entries have **null** byte count/hash and no claimed visual original review. Eleven other original PDFs are retained locally, including rejected comparison parts. No distributor figure is substituted for a missing guarantee.

New local control/monitor/bias currents are counted, and C24 remains the original digital protected charging/return network. No part in this study is physically qualified.
