# AX01 — AUX source partition and conditional POWER_RUN leaf

This private receiving study preserves P03 `db83`, E28's captured 269-part circuit, FH02 and NF05R. It implements a separate **schematic only** for an external POWER_RUN leaf. No P03 PCB or operating interface is changed. The original 90 mA AUX planning pool and TPS7A1601DGNR 100 mA output rating are retained; neither is a guaranteed fault-current limiter.

The original R113 cannot be repaired by value alone. With the actual R112 47 kΩ parallel pulldown, E28 R93 10 kΩ and complete FH/contact/wire loop allocation of 250 Ω, HIGH ≥2.4 V requires nominal R113 ≤195.399 Ω. U102's PG output is specified at only 1 mA / 0.4 V; that requires R113 ≥2397.749 Ω under the stated receiving corners. The 150 Ω trial passes the HIGH equation but overloads PG, and is explicitly rejected. U102 EN is physical pin 5 tied to VIN_IC; PG is physical pin 3 tied to internal POWER_RUN. Their roles are not interchanged.

`leaf/power_run_leaf.kicad_sch` is an actual native 14-part bench cell: 12 proposed integrated additions plus two unselected bench headers. A TPS389001 directly senses internal POWER_RUN; its open-drain output controls an NMOS and common-source back-to-back PMOS pair. A 100 Ω output resistor limits the separate leaf. Internal R113 remains 3.3 kΩ, preserving its original PG load and guard connection. Only the external J106.3 feed through FH's normally-open contact to E28 J5.3 would be changed. `integration-proposal.json` lists each proposed new reference and net change. Physical footprints, placement and copper have not been implemented or accepted.

Under explicit receiving requirements, the leaf's settled HIGH is 2.436498 V; open-circuit LOW ×1.2 is 0.39552 V. These are conditional DC comparisons. The NMOS gate HIGH is only 2.211138 V, so its −2.5 V/other-temperature table rows cannot be silently transferred. The common-source bootstrap initially obtains only AUX minus the body-diode drop. Its 1.930479 V gate-drive floor assumes a new ≤0.6 V hot low-current diode requirement and ≤1 Ω NMOS; it is **not** proved by the PMOS −2.5 V RON row. The low-VGS/hot channel, nonzero-gate OFF leakage, diode/clamp, gate charge and startup time requirements remain unaccepted. Below the supervisor's 0.8 V maximum POR boundary its output is undefined. Loaded cutoff, reverse/independent partial power and arbitrary slow supply recovery are not bounded. The native zero-ERC result does not close those functional holds.

The exact E28 native snapshot has eight LVC3G17 packages (seven additions), 22 active inputs and 21 local open-collector pullups. `generated/analysis.json` itemizes resistor paths, reference output loads, input leakage, Si8751 loads, IC no-load current and separate dynamic/nonrail allowances. Low-node pullup current already includes input current through that branch; R93, R142 and R143 are assigned to their actual upstream sources rather than charged again to E28 AUX. The six NF reference dividers are assigned once to E28's REF35 output. The inherited 90.306 µA guarded reference overlap is displayed separately; the practical partition still preserves the full original NF 11/14 mA reserve and does not spend that correction.

E28 alone is 64.973 mA healthy / 68.278 mA startup in the stated receiving scenario. It therefore does not establish the inherited 60 mA total. The 500 µA per active Schmitt-input allowance is an unaccepted transfer: the primary ΔICC row starts at 3 V, while this circuit uses approximately 2.5 V. Its healthy 60 mA fit would require ≤311.612 µA per active input under the other stated provisions, which is not presently guaranteed. Si8751's 9.5 mA row uses IN=VDD and exactly 10 kΩ TT; its 140 nA OFF current is typical, not a maximum. Actual intermediate-input, TT tolerance, dynamic/hot current and all state-transition correlations remain open. A 125°C whole-circuit guarantee is not claimed; the selected industrial Si8751 ends at 105°C and this receiving comparison uses 0–85°C.

The leaf-short columns deliberately retain the high E28 driver current before shutdown settles. They exceed the 90 mA planning pool; the 100 mA device rating alone does not establish the AUX voltage, safe current limiting, timing or thermal behavior during that transition. There is no newly accepted pool increase. Local known input capacitance is only a partial inventory; transistor charge, attached cable/input capacitance, all IC startup current and discharge paths still need physical bounds.

Native schematic exports report zero ERC findings and exact pin/value parity. Nine source/topology/current tests include the wrong TPS pin-role mutation, the rejected 150 Ω case, branch ownership and failed short-transient accounting. All current, physical, fabrication, startup, shutdown and adoption flags remain false. Original sources remain immutable and all captured bytes are listed in the handoff.
