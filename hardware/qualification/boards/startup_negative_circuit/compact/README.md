NF03C is a private **native schematic proposal**, with the two complete negative pass stages, absolute source/output sensing, independent deadlines and normally-closed gate clamps. It is **not a routed PCB, an adopted E28 modification or a build release**. `generated/current-native.json` records the fresh native checks; `generated/analysis.json` records conditional calculations. All physical, hot-SOA, startup and fabrication gates remain false.

The power order is P03 regulated source → new shunt/pass → existing E28 J1 receiving pin → original B2B bank → unchanged C8/C16 and downstream load. Source Kelvin taps are upstream of the new pass. Output taps are the actual protected capacitor nodes. BANK clamp contacts connect the actual **N19_GATE/N12_GATE after the drive resistor** to COMMON, rather than the Si8751 DRIVE outputs. No force path is allowed to depend on a retargeted sense-pad junction.

The compact design reuses E28's existing reference, source inverters, source OV checks and global fault latch. New combinational logic combines the original E28 fault request and NF requests; only the U25.10 receiving connection changes. It does not drive the J9 fault output. The returned E28_NOT_FAULT and a direct local fault mask revoke both permits/clamp release. Completion remains latched after PERMIT falls, so an output fault cannot erase its own indication. Power OFF/AUX loss clears completion. Actual E28 edits, wire faults, latch timing and reset priority still need implementation and waveform verification.

Each floating controller is supplied by a TMR1-1215 (24 V) through two 392 Ω resistors. The prior 15 V/213 Ω proposal exceeded the manufacturer's initial-feed guidance and is rejected. VIN and INTVCC have separate TPS3890 supervisors; their wired-AND status returns through an ISO7721F, with actual 5 V→AUX translation. Controller bias loss therefore enters the independent source-safe path. The full module voltage, efficiency, input surge and temperature requirements are receiving conditions, not manufacturer guarantees. In particular, the LTC4261 INTVCC voltage row uses IIN=25 mA, while this design has a different feed; transfer of that row must be tested.

The manufacturer-authored LTC4261 Rev D drawing was obtained from the explicitly recorded ICbanq mirror after direct ADI downloads failed. Page 13 was rendered and inspected. RF is in series with CR; CF connects directly from RAMP to VEE. RG+CG is a separate series branch from GATE to VEE. CF uses two current-catalogue 2.2 µF WIMA MKS2 parts per rail, nominal 7.2×7.2×13 mm. Their complete effective capacitance, hot leakage and lifetime limits remain receiving holds. They are not the older 4.7 µF/14 mm package.

HCS08/32/14 Schmitt gates receive slow comparator/watchdog edges. AUP14 cells condition external 3.3 V ARM/START and buffer loaded clamp/fault outputs. The discarded AUP08 packing retained a 200 ns/V limit despite its Schmitt marketing description. HCS continuous-AUX thresholds, absence of Ioff, and all partial-power transitions remain explicit prototype checks; no absolute maximum is an acceptance window. Source/output monitor windows are broad functional supervision, not final ±1% endpoint qualification. OPA2333's bias maximum is only specified at25 °C; the model's full-range bias is a receiving requirement. The primary input-current table/footnote also disagree (1 mA versus10 mA); use the conservative1 mA ceiling, without claiming unpowered functionality.

`generated/footprint-area.json` enumerates real library courtyards and hashes. The proposed 200×28 mm vertical envelope at front Y≈184.5 is only a placement study. It has no native board, mounting or airflow acceptance. Deep TMR/film parts must be concentrated away from the two floor inlets. Board thickness, back-side solder/THT tails and the front-wall gap need an explicit datum: Y184.5 cannot silently be both board centre and component face. Body maxima, exact lands, connector ratings, strain relief, force/quiet returns, branch protection and routes remain unimplemented. Remaining generic shunt/timing/filter parts must be selected before PCB release.

The retained digital2.4 A includes fan and NF partitions. `generated/analysis.json` keeps the normal constant-power inlet point separate from the enclosing WU heat screen. The new heat provision includes both isolated modules once; module output power is not added again. Neither result is a measured upper bound or an installed cooling pass. New source capacitor/inductor/body-diode discharge paths, source-collapse peaks, gate charge and loaded cutoff must be measured: maximum fault energy remains null. The 1 µC node charge/10 ms cutoff values are prototype receiving requirements, not primary hot guarantees.
