# Sensor I2C harness candidate

This is a concrete short sensor-branch design for the current signal PCB and the checked AS5600 board. It does not close the other I2C branches or qualify the full bus. `contract.json` binds the selected signal r4 and sensor r3 PCB hashes and extracts their actual pads. `route.scad` supplies editable route envelopes and two removable strain-relief clamps.

## Parts and termination

- Cable: 3M **3365/06**, gray, six conductors, 28 AWG stranded. Cut **82 ±1 mm**. Split the main end for 20 mm and sensor end for 12 mm, leaving nominally 50 mm intact. Trim individual split tails to the assembly jig; do not lengthen the blank or add an unmodeled loop. Use an additional 18 mm piece of the same conductor for the main ground pigtail.
- Main board populated header selection: Harwin **M20-9990445**, 0.64 mm square pins on 2.54 mm pitch, 6.1 mm mating pins and 3 mm PCB tails. This resolves the native generic `Header4` purchase specification; the native PCB footprint/pads are unchanged. Mating housing **M20-1060400**, four gold-contact **M20-1180042** crimp sockets. Use Harwin's specified tool and crimp setup; do not put three wires in one contact.
- Identify ribbon conductor 1 by its marked edge. Order across the ribbon: **1 GND, 2 SCL, 3 GND, 4 SDA, 5 GND, 6 3V3**. At the main end, join 1/3/5 to the single ground pigtail with a soldered 4 mm lap joint, maximum finished insulated diameter 3 mm. Trim those three fanout tails to 12 ±0.5 mm before stripping the 4 mm lap. Sleeve the joint with 8 mm of 3:1 adhesive-lined heat shrink, supplied ID 3 mm and recovered ID ≤1 mm; no exposed solder or stiffened copper outside the sleeve. The pigtail's opposite end receives the single ground crimp. Place this splice near (239,58,28), inside the modeled route reserve. Crimp-strip length and tooling follow the contact instruction sheet.
- Main housing cavities map by native pin number: **1 ground pigtail, 2 ribbon6/3V3, 3 ribbon2/SCL, 4 ribbon4/SDA**. The housing is unpolarized: mark cavity1 and D_J1010 pin1, inspect the net mapping before power, and never identify order only by viewing direction.
- Sensor end: ribbon1→W1, ribbon6→W2, ribbon2→W3, ribbon4→W4. Cut ground guards3/5 to 2 mm beyond the intact ribbon, individually cover with 5 mm insulating heat-shrink caps, and secure them within the fanout reserve. They remain grounded at the main end. Insert the four active tails from the sensor PCB back. Bare length approximately 3.3 mm, final tip ≤0.5 mm beyond the component face; keep backside wire/solder projection ≤1.5 mm and front fillets ≤0.6 mm. No header substitution is allowed on these solder-wire pads.

The main board top is Z8.35, from the actual 6.75 mm support datum plus 1.6 mm board. The selected header's 2.54 mm insulator seats the 14 mm mate at nominal Z10.89..24.89; this must be confirmed on received parts. Its 0.64 mm square diagonal fits the native 1 mm holes. The native old connector envelope is conservative and is not treated as a measured mating height.

## Route and support assembly

The modeled cable corridor follows (234,55,28) → (246,62,28) → (283,76,28) → (293,82,25.1), with a 4.5 mm-radius envelope. The intact ribbon starts near the second point and ends near (292,81.5,25.4); the main and sensor fanouts occupy the ends. The envelope accommodates the 7.874 mm maximum specified ribbon width and nominal thickness with placement allowance. Round bends smoothly, targeting ≥5 mm bend radius; this is an assembly design constraint, not a published 3M minimum bend rating. Do not crease the ribbon or route loose excess outside the envelope.

Two printed lower saddles use existing signal-board mounting holes **D_H2 (250,57)** and **D_H5 (294,88)**, with 4.5 mm bearing diameters. Replace the original screws with **M2×25** and **M2×20**, respectively, retaining the original underside M2 nuts and 0.5 mm washers. The added support heights are 15 and 10 mm; screw tips therefore remain at the original nominal Z−1.15. Head/washer OD ≤4.5 mm and head height ≤2 mm. No new housing or PCB holes are needed. Screwdriver access is relieved above both mounting seats.

Each ribbon clamp has a separate upper lid and two **M2×5 pan-head screws**, head OD ≤3.8 mm and height ≤1.6 mm. Tap the 1.6 mm lower-jaw pilots M2; nominal thread engagement is 2.5 mm. Clamp slot width is 8.4 mm and height 1 mm. Fit one custom-cut **6×8×0.125 mm silicone film, Shore A40 ±5**, to each jaw face; verify received cable thickness, slot dimensions and grip on a coupon. The pads provide compliant grip without assuming a quantified clamp force. Print orientation, layer adhesion, screw torque and retention are receiving/physical qualification gates. Hand tighten; do not use the solder joints to resist cable pull.

Install underside nuts and board first, replace the two mount screws with the saddle assemblies, fit ribbon and pads, then fit lids. Route and trim the split tails on a jig using the actual connector and sensor positions. Crimp and continuity-check the main end, solder the sensor end, then inspect the four-wire fanout before fitting the sensor carrier. The cable must remain slack between clamps and terminals. Record terminal pull/grip results before a powered build; no fatigue/vibration rating is asserted.

## Electrical bound and remaining gates

3M publishes **47.5 pF/m** for its unbalanced ground–signal–ground test arrangement. With a maximum 83 mm blank, the whole-length nominal estimate is **3.9425 pF per signal**. That estimate is not a guaranteed maximum and does not establish the fanout/connector capacitance.

The complete assembled branch, including connector, ground splice, split tails and solder-wire ends, has an explicit **receiving acceptance ceiling of 12 pF per SCL or SDA line**. Measure with the harness disconnected from both boards, the other signal and all non-tested conductors tied to ground, using a compensated fixture; record instrument uncertainty and require the upper uncertainty limit ≤12 pF. Reject or redesign a failing branch. The ceiling is conditional on that test; no measurement or manufacturer maximum is claimed.

The prior proposed 150 pF bus allocation remains: main-board/devices80, missing codec/future routing10, sensor15, Trill15, this harness12, all remaining harnesses18 pF. At a worst allocated 2020 Ω pullup this gives 256.7319 ns nominal RC rise time. **Other harness routes and the board capacitance allocations remain unclosed.** Final stackup/device loading, ground/analog noise, VOL and assembled 400 kHz waveforms must pass before electrical release. Firmware stays at400 kHz; reducing speed is not used to hide loading.

Source specifications establish the candidate parts, not qualification of this assembly.

## Verification

Selected local evidence is `trial-r6/review.json` plus `review.json`: 18 native cases pass, and each of the four printable clamp pieces is a single connected watertight solid. Checks cover route/components, mate/neighboring components, shell, carrier, existing control allocations, full lever/spring sweep, clamp/fastener clearances and intended washer seating. The 0.01 mm washer contact band is deliberately excluded from the empty-intersection test and separately tested for positive contact. `preview-r6.png` shows the native meshes. Latest replacement controls/LED holders/cooling require the parent's combined-assembly check; these local checks do not imply that integration has already run.

Historical r3 exposed screw-head interference; r4 added tool access but its empty test caught zero-volume coincident washer seats. r5 separated intended seat contact from interference and increased the cable placement envelope. r6 adds neighboring-component mating and full moving-lever checks. Only current r6 is selected. The cable corridor is a placement envelope, not an exact manufactured ribbon solid or a proof of pull strength.
