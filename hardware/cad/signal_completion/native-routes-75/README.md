# Left ADC common-mode clamp connection

The 10 kΩ series resistor A_R113 connects to the left common-mode clamp A_D101 through two 0.60/0.30 mm vias. The route uses the rear and In5 copper layers with an In6 ground reference. The common-mode input at A_U101 pin 2 still needs a connection. KiCad verifies 51 remaining connections.
