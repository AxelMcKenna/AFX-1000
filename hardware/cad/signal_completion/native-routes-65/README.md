# Bypass timer enable

A_U606 pins 1 and 11 connect through 4.71 mm of 0.20 mm rear copper. LINK_INPUT_READY stays on the rear layer. The coil-enable trace uses In5.Cu and one new 0.60/0.30 mm through via. Ground copper on In6.Cu references all three routes. The whole-board connectivity check verifies the timer join and preserves every other pad group. There are 62 open connections.
