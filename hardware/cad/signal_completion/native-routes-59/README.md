# Left preamp output to the ADC buffer

A_R407 pin 2 connects to A_U103 pin 3 through 31.47 mm of copper and two conventional vias. The surface escapes and inner trace have continuous adjacent ground up to the via clearances. The nearby 0.20 mm left ADC bias trace takes a short detour on the back layer and retains its original endpoints and grounded reference. KiCad verifies 68 remaining connections. Installed crosstalk and audio noise require measurement.
