# Right ADC differential filter capacitor

A_C117 pin 1 connects to A_R120 pin 1 through 1.68 mm of signal trace and one 0.60/0.30 mm via. Adjacent ground continues to the via clearance on both sides. The left ADC power-down trace keeps its two endpoints and its existing branch on In3.Cu. The negative terminal of the capacitor still needs routing. KiCad verifies 66 remaining connections.
