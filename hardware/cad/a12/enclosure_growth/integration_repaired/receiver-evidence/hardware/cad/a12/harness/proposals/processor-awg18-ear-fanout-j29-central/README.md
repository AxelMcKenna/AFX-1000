# J29 central angle-zero repair proposal

This private candidate moves the same three-pin J29 to world pin 1 `(105,76)` mm, angle 0°. Its actual native pads are `(175,134)`, `(175,136.54)`, `(175,139.08)` mm. Pin assignments stay AGND / POT_RAW_09 / V3V3_ANALOG. The serialized courtyard plus inherited 0.5 mm service allowance is world `[102.73,73.73..107.27,83.35]`; the native cached polygon is inset by 0.005 mm per side. The complete service/lead/hardware check must use the serialized bounds.

The earlier proposed angle −90° at world `(104,78.5)` crosses the MCU_PERMIT and BCLK bottom traces with its plated holes. `native_constraints.py` preserves those two native copper findings and verifies the chosen lands plus 0.25 mm clearance halo against actual foreign tracks. No clock or permit route is changed. The original first native routing attempt in `generated/history/initial-raw-pad-crossing/` records an explicit RAW09 trace crossing J29.3; the corrected escape bends to X176.5 before proceeding south.

Exactly 51 old RAW09/AVDD segments are removed and five replacement segments added, with no new vias. RAW09 connects its existing In2 anchor at `(172.5,146.5)` through `(176.5,146.5)` and `(176.5,136.54)` to J29.2. The front AVDD trunk ends at its last retained header X229, and a front branch connects J29.3 at X175. Every other segment, barrel, footprint, quiet filter, component, schematic pin map, outline, stackup and H9 exclusion is preserved. The full machine-readable object ledger is `generated/design-change.json`.

`generated/native.json` holds current raw full ERC, all-severity DRC, schematic parity and connectivity counts, with source and artifact hashes. `generated/current-native.json` indexes the sealed proofs. Native H9 all-four-layer copper exclusion and actual ear flood/cut topology are recomputed from this candidate's filled board. These are electrical and geometric diagnostics. Prior PDN, thermal, whole-body, harness and service clearances are not inherited. Panel-pot wire length, signal/noise performance, material/process/contact resistance, effective capacitance, startup, whole-unit cooling, fabrication and physical qualification remain open.

The private fanout allocation remains Seed 1 A + LED 0.764 A + bypass 0.140 A + monitor 0.080 A = 1.984 A; 0.416 A reserve has no installed endpoint. J1 stays an unfitted service proposal, and the two target fanout boards still lack native PCBs. Existing frozen candidates and canonical boards are unchanged.
