AFX-A12-R01 contains real machined copper return bars, insulating retainers and selected fastening envelopes. The analog and digital routes have separate copper paths with flat terminal landing faces. Their exact centreline coordinates and Z profile are in [design.json](design.json); nominal terminal planes are Z11.6 for E28 and Z13.1 for P03.

The bars use a 6×3.5 mm bare-copper section, 10 mm landing disks and 4 mm bolt holes. The ANA ramp-end has a full-depth round outside corner; its underside follows the lower incoming ramp envelope and its top stays level with the high terminal. Actual OCC slices of the minimum-dimension copper include both hole necks,segment centres and bend bisectors. The selected bright tin process adds up to 0.020 mm to the finished envelope and needs provider/terminal/lifetime approval. The one-dimensional DC screen excludes current crowding, contacts, solder and PCB resistance; it is not an assembled resistance guarantee.

Two machined MCAM Ketron1000 natural unfilled PEEK posts sit at world (166,113) and (166,133.125). Heads start at Z8.7, above the P03 board-thickness corner, and stay within the narrow board gap. The sloped seats and cap undersides follow the copper ramp; flat cap upper faces provide perpendicular screw-bearing surfaces. Caps retain the copper without drilling it and keep a nominal 0.30 mm gap, with a 0.15 mm minimum project allocation. These gaps and trial torques require metrology and creep/life tests.

Native collision evidence uses actual source-hashed E28/P03 courtyards and explicit conservative height classes, with an extra ±0.65 mm XY and ±0.1 mm Z bar envelope.
