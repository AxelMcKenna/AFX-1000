# Screw-retained RGB ring and populated emitter board

**Guide correction:** use `../connected-guide-r1` final guide exports and `../connected_guide.scad` integration wrappers. Earlier guide exports remain historical because their ring relief left a loose central island. The final guides are each one connected solid; all50 button seats remain verified.

Eight Kingbright APTF1616LSEEZGKQBKC common-anode RGB emitters align to the existing eight optical rods at radius 24 mm about (244,78). Exact primary package, polarity and recommended land geometry are retained in `APTF1616LSEEZGKQBKC.pdf`. Pin 1 is anode, pins 2/3/4 are red/green/blue cathodes. Each cathode branch includes a Yageo RC0603FR-071KP 1 kΩ resistor; the 24 resistors use the existing KiCad 0603 land pattern. Manufacturer resistor dimensions/rating are retained in `Yageo-RC-P.pdf` and the exact part is documented at https://www.yageogroup.com/component-documentation/download/specsheet/RC0603FR-071KP .

Only the existing three ring sink channels 93–95 are used. The three channels each feed eight resistor/LED branches; there are no new independently addressable ring segments. At the existing approximately 3 mA channel setting, equal sharing would give 0.375 mA per LED. The resistors reduce imbalance but do not guarantee equal brightness. Actual forward-voltage bins, diffuser material, ambient visibility, color balance and current-sink compliance must be tested. Native connectivity and DRC are not powered optical acceptance.

Board bottom/top are Z39.3/40.9. LED maximum envelope top is Z41.8; the retained optical rod bottoms are Z42.0. The board's main outer radius is 29.5 mm, with three mounting lobes; inner radius is 17.8 mm. Three existing-pattern M2 fasteners are newly located at radius 28.5 mm, angles 22.5°,157.5°,286°. These are explicit new lid holes, not presumed PCB holes. Use M2×12 screws, head OD≤4.5 mm, AF4×1.6 mm nuts, and 0.5 mm washers. Screw heads bear on the new 2 mm lens ears at Z47.6; nuts occupy Z37.2–38.8. Printed frame posts clamp the board against the washers. The clear lens is captured by the same three screws; no adhesive is relied upon.

The old lid treats the eight ring-light openings as generic knob holes, producing Ø9.5 mm holes rather than the intended Ø3 mm. The new lid repairs only these holes using a 2D annular fill and recuts Ø3 mm receivers. The old guide already has explicit Ø3.2 rod holes; the new guide additionally clears the actual carrier/board outline by 0.2 mm. Exact symmetric-difference checks prove all geometry outside declared ring aperture/mount/carrier masks is preserved, including earlier display, selector, meter and bank changes.

`native-r6` passes nine empty clearance/preservation checks, three positive retention/material-restoration checks, and eight printable full/split exports. `pcb-native-r5/review.json` records zero native KiCad violations/opens and verifies every emitter/resistor/wire net group. `wiring.csv` maps four leads to the actual integrated signal board. Integration wrappers: `tbh06_ring_installed()`, `tbh06_lid_variant()`, `tbh06_guide_variant()`.

Failed artifacts remain historical: the original annular board hit scene-holder posts; an attachment arm hit one resistor solder reserve; a whole-boundary retention intersection and the original near-coincident lid fill produced invalid facets. Bounded contact probes and a 2D annular fill corrected these findings. A CGAL lid diagnostic timed out at 60 seconds and was not repeated; the repaired Manifold lid passed a bounded probe before the full batch. Final source bindings are in `checkpoint-sha256.json` and `verification.json`.

Receiving gates: actual PCB thickness, printed pocket/rod clearances, screw/nut dimensions, usable mounting torque, clear optical material, solder insulation, and complete wire routing/strain relief. The local four-wire reserves extend only 2.5 mm below the board. This is a modeled assembly candidate, not fabrication release or whole-unit acceptance.
