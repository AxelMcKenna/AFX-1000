# Meter and bank emitter checkpoint

This follow-on implements two 20-emitter meter boards and four copies of one bank-emitter board. The 44 sink channels are bound to the existing integrated signal PCB by `wiring.csv` and its source-hashed review. All emitters are Kingbright KPT-1608CGCK with manufacturer-derived lands, polarity, body tolerances and optical locations. This green population establishes physical fit; final meter color progression and brightness remain optical acceptance work.

Each board is retained with M2 screws, washers and nuts. The printed opaque frame separates meter cells and holds the retained lens flange against the lid. Meter boards occupyZ39.3–40.9; bank boardsZ39.6–41.2. Meter mounts are(43.9,14.75),(100.9,14.75),(43.9,41.75),(100.9,41.75). Bank mounts areX76/95/114/133, each atY97 and 102. Use M2×10 screws with head OD≤4.5, M2 nuts AF4×1.6 and 0.5 mm washers. Meter nuts beginZ37.2, bank nutsZ37.5. Confirm actual hardware and printed stack before assembly.

`meters-r4` passes seven empty clearances (including full button travel and all 42 local wire exits), two retention probes and two printable frames. `banks-r4` passes seven empty clearances, two retention probes and seven printable exports including combined full/split lid and guide. The combined exports include sealed TBH04 display/selector modifications. Existing lenses are retained. `pcb-native-r3/review.json` records zero native DRC violations and zero unconnected items for all three distinct routed board designs. This is board connectivity verification, not schematic ERC or powered qualification.

`banks-r3/led_guide` is retained failed evidence: CGAL emitted two degenerate collinear facets and four bad directed edges. Re-exporting with the pinned Manifold backend passed the same closed-oriented-boundary check in r4; no geometry was weakened to bypass the check. Earlier bank r1 caught incomplete imported button-parameter scope; `button_motion.scad` now imports through the complete housing parameter scope.

For integration use `tbh 05_bank_meter_installed()`, `tbh 05_bank_meter_lid()` and `tbh 05_bank_meter_guide()` from `banks.scad`. The installed module includes purchased-body and local lead reserves for collision review. Frame exports are print-oriented; installed modules use housing datums.

Anode leads need rated harness splices, not several conductors in one crimp. Local wire envelopes reserve only the first 2.5 mm below each board. Full harness routing, strain relief, solder insulation and lid-service loops remain separate gates. Tiny boards and .4 mm cell walls require fabrication/print capability confirmation. There is no physical or optical acceptance claim. Button-indicator and RGB-ring carriers are not implemented by this checkpoint and must not be inferred from the 44 implemented channels.
