Manufacturer dimensions are retained in `../led_follow_on/reference/PTV09.pdf`, page 3; shaft-flat dimensions are onpage 2. The originalØ6.2 blind 10 mm socket cannot accept this shaft. The 5 mm threaded bushing also protrudes 0.2 mm into the old knob underside at its 2.8 mm standoff. `knobs.scad` explicitly corrects both interfaces:Ø10×1 mm underside counterbore andØ6.2×13 mm blind shaft socket. Small knobs become 15.5 mm tall, preserving diameters and panel standoff. No shaft cutting is required.

Shaft tip is 60.2±0.5; insertion is 11.8±0.5, leaving at least 0.7 mm to the blind end. The 25 mm shaft has a 12 mm flat; the set screw atZ54.4 engages it throughout the stated shaft-length range. Tap theØ2.5 radial pilot M3 and use a suitable short screw bearing on the flat; screw selection, torque and plastic-thread durability require physical validation. The circular clearance bore itself does not retain the knob.

Supplied washer is 0.4 mm thick, OD15±1; nut thickness 2±0.2, AF11±0.5. Worst drawn stack endsZ48.2,0.2 mm below the knob. The 5 mm bushing ends 48.6; the counterbore ends 49.4, giving 0.8 mm axial recess margin. Unspecified bushing/panel/print tolerances are not hidden in these nominal figures: measure them and confirm free rotation and thread engagement before assembly. The manufacturer’s supplied hardware is required; arbitrary thicker hardware can invalidate the stack.

Native verification checks max shaft/bushing/washer/nut solids against the revised knobs and exports four printable sizes. Existing housing holes remainØ9.5. This is a shaft receiving correction, not complete pot installation verification: new shoulder/body placement, lead dressing, antirotation and full integrated carrier clearances still require review. No sourcing or physical acceptance is claimed for the center-detent candidate.

The minimum knob height is 15.5 mm: former 13 mm knobs gain 2.5 mm; former 15 mm knobs gain 0.5 mm; 18 mm knobs stay unchanged. All diameters and locations are unchanged.
