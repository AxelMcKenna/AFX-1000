# Rail disconnect normal-state logic

E_U22 pin 10 reaches the logic group at E_U23 pin 9 through 29.11 mm of 0.20 mm copper. The bottom and In2 traces reference In6 and In1 ground. Two local ground stitching vias accompany the signal transitions.

KiCad verified the intended pad-group join. The board has 848 open connections.
