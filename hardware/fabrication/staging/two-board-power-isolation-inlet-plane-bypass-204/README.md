# Inlet-plane clearance at E_U1

Two 2.50 mm keepouts remove inlet copper from the contact gap on In2 and In3. The eight-layer survey records 19 package-midline crossings, down from 21. The other E_U1 crossings remain open.

The inlet planes widen by 4.00 mm along the local bypass. On both layers and both meshes, resistance between the modeled inlet and load contacts decreases by 0.167% to 0.292%. The sheet model excludes barrels, temperature rise and current sharing between layers. All named pad groups match the previous board, with 1,053 connections remaining.
