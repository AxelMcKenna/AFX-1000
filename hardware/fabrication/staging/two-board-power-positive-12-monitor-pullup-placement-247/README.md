# Positive 12 V overvoltage monitor pull-up

E_R69 moves to (23.50, 129.75) mm on the rear side. Rear copper connects its first terminal to the 2.5 V auxiliary regulator distribution and its second terminal to the P12_OV_OK monitor. The two 0.25 mm traces total 1.62 mm. KiCad verified both joins and every existing pad partition. The regulator reaches 201 of 230 assigned pads. The power board has 955 open connections.
