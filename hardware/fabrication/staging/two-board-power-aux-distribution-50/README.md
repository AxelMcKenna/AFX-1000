# Auxiliary regulator distribution

Five 0.40 mm surface branches connect the groups at N_R8, N_C84, N_U47, N_C90 and N_C94 to regulator output P_U102 pin 1. Each branch has an adjacent ground reference after copper refill. The component positions and via locations match the preceding revision.

The regulator reaches 85 of 230 assigned pads, including 21 of 81 IC supply pins. The rail still has 105 open connections. Loaded current and regulator temperature remain unverified.

KiCad reports 1,516 open connections across the power board, zero geometry or schematic parity findings and two isolated-reference ERC warnings. The paired placement matches the assembly capture. New solder mask and paste plots reproduce the prior measured via clearances.
