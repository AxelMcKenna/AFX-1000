# LT3080 positive 12 V output bypass capacitor

P_C43 moves to the front beside the positive 12 V regulator output tab. Its 100 nF capacitor uses a 1.77 mm connection to the tab and a 1.40 mm return to a dedicated ground via. The larger output capacitor and the rail distribution still require routing. Supplier height and physical enclosure fit remain unqualified. KiCad verifies 2 intended joins and 1,320 remaining open connections.
