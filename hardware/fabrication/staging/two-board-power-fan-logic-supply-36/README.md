# Fan logic supply connection

A 4.12 mm front-layer trace joins the 5 V supply pin of the SN74LVC1G74 flip-flop F_U8 to the 100 nF capacitor F_C202. The trace is 0.30 mm wide and uses the In1.Cu ground plane as its reference. Both the capacitor and flip-flop have ground connections. Native refill confirms full trace coverage and equivalent ground and power-plane fills. KiCad confirms 1,599 remaining opens. Upstream rail distribution and loaded verification remain open.
