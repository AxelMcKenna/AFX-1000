# Positive rail overvoltage divider ground

E_R58 pin 2 provides the ground reference for the +19 V overvoltage sense divider. Its 5.41 mm front-layer trace reaches analog ground on In5.Cu through one 0.60/0.30 mm via. KiCad verifies 1 pad-group join and 1,135 remaining connections.
