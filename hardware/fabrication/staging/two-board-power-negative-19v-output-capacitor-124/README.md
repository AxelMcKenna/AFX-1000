# Negative 19 V regulator output capacitor

The 22 µF capacitor P_C52 sits behind P_U10. Its supply reaches the front bypass capacitor through 1.15 mm of rear copper, one 0.60 mm via and 1.72 mm of front copper. A 6.32 mm rear trace returns P_C52 to the same ground via that serves the bypass capacitor. Both supply and return traces are 0.60 mm wide. KiCad verifies two pad-group joins and 1,253 remaining connections. Regulator stability and thermal performance still require measurement.
