# Negative 19 V input-valid supervisor divider

N_R65 connects to the sense input of N_U21 through 2.49 mm of copper and one via. Its return connects to the local PWR_N19_SOURCE via through 2.91 mm of bottom-layer copper. Both paths use 0.30 mm traces. The upper divider arm and powered threshold measurement remain outstanding.

KiCad verified the two intended pad-group joins. The board has 859 remaining open connections. [Resistor placement and enclosure clearances](component-placement.json)
