# Negative 12 V regulator output bypass

The 100 nF capacitor P_C58 sits beside the 22 µF output capacitor P_C57 on the back of the board. P_C58 connects to the regulator output through 1.14 mm of 0.50 mm copper and to the local return through 1.14 mm of 0.40 mm copper. KiCad verifies two pad-group joins and 1,220 remaining connections. Regulator stability and thermal performance still require measurement.
