# Negative 12 V regulator output capacitor

The 22 µF output capacitor P_C57 sits behind LT3090 regulator P_U11. Pin 1 connects to the capacitor through 0.82 mm of front copper, one 0.60 mm via with a 0.30 mm drill, and 0.74 mm of rear copper. The regulator and capacitor return to the same ground via. KiCad verifies three pad-group joins and 1,257 remaining connections. Regulator stability and thermal performance still require measurement.
