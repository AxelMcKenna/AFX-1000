# LT3080 positive 12 V output capacitor

The 22 µF output capacitor P_C42 moves beside the regulator on the rear. Two parallel 0.80 mm traces and two through vias join its positive land to the output tab. Its return reaches an existing system ground via. Digital checks verify copper geometry, ground continuity and both pad joins. Loaded regulator stability and ceramic capacitance at bias remain to be measured. Supplier height and physical enclosure fit remain unqualified.
