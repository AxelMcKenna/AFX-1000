# Lower negative regulator feedback divider

P_C75 pin 2 ties to P_R46 pin 2 and P_R47 pin 1 through two short rear traces. A local ground via connects their In6.Cu reference copper to the In1.Cu ground plane. KiCad verifies 1,168 remaining connections.

|---|---|---|---:|
| 0.25 | P_R47:1 to P_C75:2 | B.Cu | 2.11 |
| 0.25 | P_C75:2 to P_R46:2 | B.Cu | 1.09 |
