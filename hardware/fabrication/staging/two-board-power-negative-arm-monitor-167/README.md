# Connect the negative rail arm monitor

The 0.20 mm In5.Cu trace joins the conditioned −12 V rail arm signal at the monitor header to the logic group at N_U41 pin 12. The trace uses the adjacent In6.Cu ground plane and existing plated connections. KiCad verifies 1 pad-group joins and 1,136 remaining connections.

|---|---|---|---:|
| 0.20 | N_J1:24 to N_U41:12 | In5.Cu | 30.20 |
