# Negative rail feedback divider ground

P_R41 pin 2 provides the ground reference for the −12 V feedback divider. Its 9.06 mm front-layer trace reaches analog ground on In1.Cu through one 0.60/0.30 mm via. KiCad verifies 1 pad-group join and 1,118 remaining connections.
