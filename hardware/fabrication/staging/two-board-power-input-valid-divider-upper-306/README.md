# Negative 19 V input-valid sense divider

N_R64 pin 2 connects directly to N_R65 pin 1 on the bottom layer. The 1.10 mm sense trace uses 0.30 mm copper over the In6 ground plane and shares the existing connection to N_U21 pin 1. The N19_VIN feed to N_R64 pin 1 remains open.

KiCad verified the intended pad-group join. The board has 856 remaining open connections. [Resistor placement and enclosure clearances](component-placement.json)
