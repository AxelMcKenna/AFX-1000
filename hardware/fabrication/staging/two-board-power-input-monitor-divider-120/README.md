# Input voltage monitor divider

The input-voltage monitor divider midpoint connects P_R309 to P_R308 through a 0.20 mm trace on In5.Cu and two surface connections. The divider passes under the digital monitor traces. Local ground copper backs the route and connects its layer transitions to the ground plane. KiCad verifies one intended pad-group join and 1,269 remaining connections.
