# Raw positive converter soft-start controller connection

P_U1 pin 9 joins P_C13 and P_R6 on the raw positive converter soft-start node. The two 0.25 mm traces total 1.35 mm. A ground via 1.35 mm from the signal via connects their two reference layers.

KiCad verified the expected pad groups. The board has 903 remaining open connections.
