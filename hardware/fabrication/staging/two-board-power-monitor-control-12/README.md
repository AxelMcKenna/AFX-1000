# Rail monitor and protection control routing

Fourteen surface connections join comparator inputs, reference dividers, timeout capacitors and negative-rail control components. These 0.20 mm traces range from 7.08 to 21.24 mm and use no vias. The new copper clears the enclosure cradle contacts and stays over the adjacent ground field throughout.

KiCad 10.0.7 reports 1,720 native opens, down from 1,734. All fourteen intended pad-group joins survive a full zone refill. Saved and refilled boards have zero geometry and schematic parity findings. All 47 zone-layer fields match the corresponding parent fields. ERC retains two isolated-reference warnings. The board keeps the placement used for the housing review. Loaded electrical performance and manufacturing constraints still require verification.
