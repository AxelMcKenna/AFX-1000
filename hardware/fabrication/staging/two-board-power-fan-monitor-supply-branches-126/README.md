# Fan monitor supply connections

Three 0.40 mm branches distribute the 3.3 V supply to the fan-monitor resistor and capacitor groups. Each branch uses two 0.60 mm vias with 0.30 mm drills. The supply now reaches 24 of 31 assigned lands. KiCad verifies three pad-group joins and 1,248 remaining connections. The adjacent ground plane was checked under the full trace width. Fan monitoring still requires functional and noise measurements.
