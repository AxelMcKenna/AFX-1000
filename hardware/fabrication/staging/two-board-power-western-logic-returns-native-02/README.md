# TBP66 western logic grounds and timeout reference repair

This working candidate closes **21 ground connections**, including 13 previously disconnected logic GND pins. Power-board opens fall from 2,063 at [TBP65](../two-board-power-logic-ground-access-native-01/README.md) to **2,042**. Open the [KiCad project](power_protection.kicad_pro).

The [plan](../two-board-western-logic-returns-plan-02/plan.json) extends the main In1/In6 reference fields in three western regions. It adds 17 tracks and 39 ground vias, and replaces 28 In1 segments of E28_TIMEOUT_RAW with three In2 segments using the existing endpoint vias. Two nearby ground stitches connect the layer-transition references, at 0.83 and 0.99 mm from the signal vias. The replacement removes a gap in ground coverage beneath F_U11's ground escape. Component positions, board geometry and schematic connectivity remain unchanged.

[Native validation](review.json) reports zero geometry, parity and ERC errors, with two retained ERC warnings. The [independent audit](western-logic-returns-review.json) proves exactly 21 ground-group merges, preservation of all other native connectivity, all 2,288 retained reference cases, all 14 new paths, all 39 via contacts and both signal transition stitches. The [whole timeout-net survey](../two-board-western-timeout-return-survey-01/survey.json) finds the net connected and all its tracks referenced. The [prior control-net survey](../two-board-control-return-survey-after-07/survey.json) still records four existing connector-end reference gaps and no new gaps.

Nine supply fields and all seven quiet-return networks remain exact. One short ground escape at E_U37 adds a single bounded clearance hole to each of the two DC-input sheets. Each loses 0.791496 mm² of copper and retains its connected component count. The [conditional sheet comparison](../two-board-western-inlet-via-comparison-02/review.json) estimates a 0.0323% increase in paired sheet resistance on the finest unshifted grid; resistance refinement and grid-shift differences are below 2%. Exact model-input equality permits reuse of the original computed comparison after the timeout repair. This does **not** establish current capacity, peak physical current density, temperature rise or an installed thermal rating. The [fill record](../two-board-power-western-logic-returns-filled-04/local-plane-fill.json) keeps all 39 other authored fills exactly, including the original supply fill affected by native rounding.

Twenty native layer details were inspected: [western grounds](plots/western-grounds-inspection.png), [fan grounds](plots/fan-grounds-inspection.png), [timeout route](plots/timeout-ground-inspection.png) and [input clearance](plots/inlet-ground-inspection.png). See the [visual review](visual-review.json).

The [housing compatibility check](../../../cad/two_board_print_02/latest-physical-compatibility-TBP66-candidate.json) verifies exact equality of all 1,101 complete footprint records, board general settings and Edge.Cuts against the sealed housing's TBP61 basis. The housing remains an unpowered fit prototype.

**Not ready for fabrication.** The power board has 2,042 opens and the signal board has 251. The approved ESS codec footprint, remaining routing and grounds, AUX distribution, current/thermal design, loaded startup/fault behavior, final mechanical carriers/harness, accepted stackup, exact BOM and manufacturing outputs remain unfinished.
