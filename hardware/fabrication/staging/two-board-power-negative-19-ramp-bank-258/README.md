# Negative 19 V ramp capacitor bank

The 0.40 mm In2 trace joins the ramp terminals of N_C26 and N_C27. Adjacent In3 copper connects both capacitor returns to one another on PWR_N19_SOURCE. This floating bank still needs its controller and source feeds.
